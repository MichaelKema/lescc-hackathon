# LESCC bid files

The Lower East Side Cultural Center (LESCC) team's proposal for the Allen Street concession, written as JSON so the model and the story can read it.

- These files are the team's plan, not data collected from outside sources
- Written 2026-11-21 during the LESCC hackathon; raw files stay unedited, per the project README
- Numbers that still need an outside source are listed in `../README.md`, section 6

## `lescc-community-bid.json`

The whole bid: what it is, who pays, who is on the team, and how it grows.

- `executive_summary`: the concept, Community Value Capture, replacing a single food stall with a cultural anchor
- `financial_contribution_framework`: merchant pledge fund, LES BID backstop, Park Improvement District (PID) pilot, economic rationale
- `team_structure_and_governance`: team members with their roles, and plans to widen the coalition
- `public_venue_and_programming`: restored building and restrooms, community events, metrics to track
- `organizational_transition_roadmap`: three phases, from pledge coalition to BID backstop to PID pilot

## `lescc-financial-channels.json`

Four ways the bid routes neighborhood revenue to NYC Parks license fees.

- `mechanism_1` Direct Coalition Fee Guarantee: signed merchant pledges cover the minimum fee and 25% security deposit
- `mechanism_2` LES BID Backstop: mandatory assessment on property owners, following the Bryant Park Corporation model
- `mechanism_3` PID Pilot: a park-specific assessment district, pursued with Council Member Marte
- `mechanism_4` Closed Data Loop: merchant benefits plus audit metrics that show return and keep pledges renewing

## How the project can use them

The files feed the proposal side of the model and the "what changes" part of the story.

- Revenue: who guarantees the license fee and deposit in the proposal scenario
- Social and cultural capital: foot traffic, storefront vacancy, business openings and closings, and event attendance are candidate proxies
- Story: the coalition and the roadmap give the narrative its people and its sequence
